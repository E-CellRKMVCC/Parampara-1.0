# Problem Statement: PS-AFR-03

## 📌 Problem Overview
- **Problem Code**: `PS-AFR-03`
- **Title**: Optimizing Urban Heat Mitigation and cooling strategies via Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AIML)
- **Theme**: Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- **Track / Project Type**: Software Track (Geospatial AI / Machine Learning Engine & Dashboard)
- **Organized By**: E-Cell RKMVCC — PARAMPARA 1.0

---

## 🔍 Detailed Problem Description
Develop a geospatial AI/ML-based system, backed with physics-informed decision making to identify urban heat stress hotspots, quantify key drivers of urban heating, and generate optimized, scenario-based cooling interventions for mitigating urban heat impacts.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives & Expectations
1. **Urban Heat Hotspot Identification**: Analyze Land Surface Temperature (LST), albedo, vegetation index (NDVI), and built-up density to pinpoint microclimate heat islands.
2. **Physics-Informed ML Modeling**: Integrate thermodynamic physics models (energy balance, micro-meteorology) with Machine Learning to identify main causes of temperature amplification.
3. **Scenario-Based Simulation Engine**: Simulate cooling interventions (green roofs, urban canopy restoration, cool pavements, urban water bodies) and calculate temperature reduction scores.
4. **Urban Planning Dashboard**: Provide city administration and urban developers with interactive spatial maps to evaluate cost vs. cooling efficacy of intervention scenarios.

---

## 🛠️ Suggested Technical Stack
- **Geospatial & Remote Sensing**: Landsat 8/9 Thermal Infrared (TIRS), MODIS, Sentinel-2, Google Earth Engine, GeoPandas.
- **AI/ML & Physics Integration**: PyTorch / TensorFlow, Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), XGBoost / Random Forest Regressors.
- **Backend & Data Pipeline**: Python (FastAPI / GeoDjango), Raster processing pipeline.
- **Frontend / GIS Visualization**: React, Deck.gl / Mapbox GL JS / CesiumJS for 3D/2D urban thermal visualization.
