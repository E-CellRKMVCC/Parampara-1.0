## 🔍 Detailed Problem Description
Teams may define their own problem statement addressing a pressing real-world issue in campus life, civic administration, or local community, provided it does not strictly fit the above 7 predefined themes.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives & Expectations
1. **Clear Real-World Problem Definition**: Identify and validate a distinct, high-impact societal, campus, or civic challenge.
2. **Innovative Solution Architecture**: Design a scalable tech-enabled solution (Software app, IoT hardware, AI pipeline, or Hybrid system).
3. **User & Stakeholder Impact**: Clearly define the target beneficiary group and quantify potential economic, social, or environmental impact.
4. **Implementation Feasibility**: Demonstrate technical proof-of-concept feasibility, operational viability, and deployment roadmap.

---

## 🛠️ Suggested Technical Stack
- **Open Stack**: Teams are free to select any combination of programming languages, frameworks, AI/ML tools, hardware microcontrollers, cloud infrastructure, or database engines suitable for their proposed solution.

## 📊 Recommended PPT Presentation Structure
When preparing your solution PPT for `PS-MS-01`, include the following slides:

1. **Title Slide**: `PS-MS-01`, Project Title, Team Name, and Team Members.
2. **Problem Definition & Context**: Background on the chosen societal/campus issue and target users affected.
3. **Proposed Innovation**: Core solution idea, key features, and unique value proposition.
4. **Technical Architecture & Flow**: System block diagram, hardware/software workflow, and data flow.
5. **UI / Concept Mockups**: Application design screens, hardware schematics, or process flowcharts.
6. **Feasibility, Impact & Business Model**: Sustainability plan, scalability metrics, and expected social ROI.
